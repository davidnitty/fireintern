- narrative_strength × +0.25 - liquidity/TVL × +0.20 - market_conditions × +0.10 - holder_count × +0.10 - chart_structure × +0.10
Verdict: BUY (>0.5), WAIT (>0.2), DYOR (>0), PASS (≤0 or EXTREME risk)
DATA SOURCES: - Primary: pump.fun (REST + WebSocket via PumpPortal) - Cross-ref: DexScreener (volume/liquidity) - Safety: Rugcheck + Solscan (LP locked, mint authority) - Viral: X API v2 (engagement spikes)
